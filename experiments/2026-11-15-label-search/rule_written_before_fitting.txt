Written 2026-11-15 before any model was fit.
The NDVI model "works" only if, under leave-one-year-out validation (every year predicted by a
model that never saw it):
  1. pooled mean pinball loss (p10, p50, p90) is at least 5 % lower than the climatology benchmark, and
  2. the per-year skill is positive in at least 60 % of the years, and
  3. it is positive in at least one El Nino year and at least one La Nina year.
Otherwise the verdict is no_skill_over_climatology and the next label is tried.

GDHY maize yield (second label), written 2026-11-15 before any GDHY model was fit.
Same three conditions. Target: yield anomaly in % against each cell's own linear trend, the trend
fitted without the held-out year. Because the table is small (about 0.5 degree cells, 1995-2016),
the LightGBM settings are fixed here in advance: num_leaves 4, min_data_in_leaf 20, 150 rounds,
learning rate 0.05. They are not tuned afterwards.

GGCP10 maize production (third label), written 2026-11-15 before any GGCP10 model was fit.
Same three conditions. 2010-2020 only (11 years), so: target is the production anomaly in %
against each cell's own mean of the training years (no trend, the series is too short), cells
need at least 8 training years and production above zero in every year. LightGBM settings fixed
in advance: num_leaves 4, min_data_in_leaf 20, 150 rounds, learning rate 0.05.
